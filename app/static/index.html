Card Lifecycle State Machine

Retail banking two-phase settlement protocol

1. AUTHORIZED Hold placed on customer account (Available = Booked − Holds). Booked balance is untouched; zero ledger entries written.

2. CAPTURED Hold released and converted into an immutable double-entry transfer (Customer DEBIT − Merchant CREDIT). Supports partial captures!

3. VOIDED Pre-settlement cancellation. Hold is restored to available balance immediately. Zero ledger entries created.

4. REFUNDED Post-settlement reversal. Generates a reverse double-entry transfer (Merchant DEBIT − Customer CREDIT).
